Left alone is the autonomous loop: it picks its own moods, blinks in clusters, glances around the room and drifts a few pixels to keep from burning into the panel. Poked is the onboard BOOT button; Petted is a finger held on the touch pad for three seconds. Falling asleep is what five ignored minutes look like.

Each expression clip starts from neutral, so you can see the trick that does most of the work: the change is hidden behind a blink. The lids shut, the shape swaps, the lids open.

The weather glances are what it does every twenty minutes once it is on WiFi: six seconds of the matching face with the conditions animated around it and the temperature in the corner, then back to whatever it was doing.
